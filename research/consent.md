# Consent

Send before the session. Offer a read-aloud version or a short call to go through it. Ask for written agreement (a reply email saying "I agree" is enough) or verbal agreement on the recording at the start.

## Plain-language version

**What this is.** A 60-minute session where you try a sample checkout page with your usual assistive technology, so I can see what works and what does not.

**What it is not.** It is not a test of you. If something is hard to use, that is a fault in the page.

**What I will do.** I will ask you to try some tasks and talk through what you are doing. I will take notes. With your agreement I will record audio and your screen.

**Your choices.**
- You can skip any task or question.
- You can take a break or stop at any time, without giving a reason. You will still be paid.
- You can ask me to delete your session at any time up to [date, for example 30 days after the session].

**Your information.**
- Notes use a code (P1, P2, P3), not your name.
- Recordings stay on [where, for example an encrypted drive] and only I can open them. I will delete them on [date].
- I may write about what happened in the case study and portfolio, without your name. I will show you any description of your session or quotes before they go public. You can say no.
- I will not share your contact details.

**Payment.** [Amount], paid by [method] within [number] days.

**Contact.** [Your email]

## Agreement

I have read or heard this and my questions were answered.

- [ ] I agree to take part.
- [ ] I agree to audio and screen recording. (Optional. You can take part without it.)
- [ ] I agree that anonymous quotes may be used after I see them. (Optional.)
