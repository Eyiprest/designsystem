# Recruitment

Aim: 3 people who use a screen reader, screen magnifier, voice control or keyboard-only navigation. Mix tools if you can: for example one screen reader user, one magnifier user, one keyboard-only user. One person per tool is enough to see something. It is not enough to generalise.

## Where to ask

- Your own contacts, asked directly, with no pressure to say yes
- Local or national blind, low-vision and disability organisations (ask the organisation how it prefers to be approached)
- Accessibility communities and meetups you already belong to
- A paid recruitment service that lists assistive-technology users, if you have budget

Do not recruit through accounts or groups that you do not belong to, and do not post in support groups for patients or carers.

## Message (email or post)

> **Subject: Paid 60-minute session: trying a sample checkout page**
>
> Hello [name],
>
> I'm Eyituoyor Prest, a designer and developer. I built a small set of interface components and I want to see where a real person gets stuck using them with assistive technology.
>
> What it involves: one 60-minute video or phone call. You use your own computer and software to try a sample checkout page. Nothing real is bought, and no payment details are involved. I will ask you to talk through what you are doing.
>
> I will pay [amount] for your time, whether or not you finish the tasks. You can stop at any point.
>
> If you are interested, please answer a few questions here: [link to screener]. I will reply within [number] days. Ask me for the questions in another format if that works better for you.
>
> Thank you,
> Eyituoyor

## Selection

Pick for variety of tools and experience first, speed of reply second. Tell everyone who answers, including those you do not pick, within one week.
