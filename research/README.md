# User research kit

For 2 to 3 sessions with people who use assistive technology. **No sessions have been run. There are no participants and no findings.** Everything here is a template. Fields in [square brackets] need your input.

Three people will not show how common a problem is. They will show whether a real person gets stuck where my own checks say nobody should. Report them as "what happened in 3 sessions", never as a study with a sample.

My own keyboard and axe checks are not user research and must not be labelled that way.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `recruitment.md` | Where to find people and what to send |
| `screener.md` | Questions to pick participants |
| `consent.md` | Consent form and plain-language version |
| `session-guide.md` | 60-minute script, tasks, prompts |
| `findings-template.md` | One sheet per session |
| `prioritisation.md` | How to rank problems, with a worked example that is clearly marked as fictional |

## Rules

1. Pay people for their time. Suggest [amount, in your currency] for 60 minutes, paid whether or not they finish.
2. Ask what format and tool they want for the consent form and the session, before the session.
3. Let them use their own device, software and settings. Do not ask them to switch screen reader or browser.
4. Test the components, not the person. A failed task is a defect in the page.
5. Record only with written consent. Store notes without names. Delete recordings on the date you promised.
6. Do not publish quotes or findings without the participant's approval.
