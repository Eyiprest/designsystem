# Screener

Offer in the format the person asks for: web form, email, phone call.

1. Which of these do you use to use a computer? (select all)
   - Screen reader (which: NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, Narrator, other: ___)
   - Screen magnifier or browser zoom above 200%
   - Keyboard only, no mouse
   - Voice control (which: ___)
   - Switch or other device (___)
   - None of these
2. About how long have you used it? (under 1 year, 1 to 3 years, over 3 years)
3. Which browser do you use most? (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, other)
4. Which device will you use for the session? (Windows, Mac, other)
5. Can you share your screen and your audio on a video call? If not, would a phone call work?
6. Have you bought something online in the last 6 months? (yes, no) Used only to understand your experience. A "no" does not exclude you.
7. Do you work in design, accessibility testing or web development? (yes, no) Used to balance the mix. A "yes" does not exclude you.
8. What do you need from me to make the session comfortable? (for example: extra time, breaks, captions, a different call tool)
9. Which days and times suit you? (give a range)

Do not ask about medical conditions, diagnoses or age. The tool you use is enough.

## Scoring

No score. Choose for the mix described in `recruitment.md`. Keep answers separate from session notes. Delete answers from people you do not pick within 30 days.
