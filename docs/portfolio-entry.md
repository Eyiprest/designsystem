## Page summary (about 80 words)

Steady is a small component library I designed and built end to end: tokens, 12 components, docs and tests. A fictional checkout shows how the pieces fit together for bank transfer, USSD and cash payment instructions. It is a demonstration and takes no payment. I tested it with axe, keyboard presses and page scans, and I list what I have not tested yet: screen readers, other browsers and real users.
