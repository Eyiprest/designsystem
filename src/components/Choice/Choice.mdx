# Checkbox and radio group

One family built on native `input type="checkbox"` and `input type="radio"`, grouped in a `fieldset` with a `legend`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Radios} />

## Purpose

- **Checkbox:** a yes or no answer, or any number of choices from a group.
- **Radio group:** exactly one choice from a short list that should stay visible.

## Anatomy

1. Group legend (also names the group for assistive technology)
2. Group hint and error (optional)
3. Choice: a 24 px indicator, a label and an optional hint. The label is part of the click target.

## States

Unchecked, checked, focus, error, disabled. Checked is shown by a tick (checkbox) or a dot (radio), as well as colour. Error colours the border of unchecked indicators and adds the group error text.

## Keyboard

| Control | Keys |
|---|---|
| Checkbox | Tab to focus, Space toggles |
| Radio group | Tab enters the group once (on the checked radio, or the first). Arrow keys move and select. Tab leaves the group. |

Both checked with real key presses.

## Naming

`RadioGroup` and `CheckboxGroup` take a `legend`. A single `Checkbox` uses its own `label`. Hints and errors are linked with `aria-describedby`.

## Screen reader

Expected for a radio: "Payment method, group. Bank transfer, radio button, 1 of 3, not checked". **Not yet confirmed with a screen reader.**

## Do and don't

- Do write the legend as a question or noun: "Payment method".
- Do list radios in a sensible order and preselect nothing unless there is a safe default.
- Don't use a checkbox for an action that takes effect at once. Use Switch.
- Don't nest groups.

## Responsive

Choices stack vertically. Labels wrap beside the indicator.

## Reduced motion

No animation.

## Limitations

- No indeterminate checkbox state.
- No card-style or button-style choices.
- `RadioGroup` needs a `name`. It is also the key `Form` uses for the error message.
